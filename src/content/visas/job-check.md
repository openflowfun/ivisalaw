whoItsFor:
  lead: >-
    One Job Check covers one set of identical roles. If the duties, location or pay differ,
    that is a different Job Check.
  fits:
    - You already hold accreditation and have a specific role to fill.
    - You are hiring several people into genuinely identical positions.
    - Your previous Job Check has expired or is about to.
  doesntFit:
    - You are not yet accredited — that comes first.
    - The role differs in duties, location or pay from one you have already had approved.
    - You want to hire someone already holding an AEWV for the same role with you.

eligibility:
  - title: You must already hold accreditation
    detail: >-
      A Job Check cannot be lodged by an unaccredited business, and an approved Job Check dies
      with the accreditation it sits under. Sequence matters more here than anywhere else in
      the process.
  - title: Advertise first, for the right period
    detail: >-
      Unless the role is exempt, advertise before lodging. Fourteen days minimum at ANZSCO or
      NOL skill levels 1 to 3, and 21 days at levels 4 and 5. The advertisement itself is
      assessed, so it has to be a real listing rather than a formality.
    figure:
      value: 14 or 21 days
      label: Minimum advertising, by skill level
      source: https://www.immigration.govt.nz/work/for-employers/getting-accreditation-or-approval-to-hire/employer-accreditation-for-the-aewv/applying-for-a-job-check-process-steps/
  - title: What the advertisement has to say
    detail: >-
      Job title and location, a description covering the key tasks, duties and responsibilities,
      and the minimum and maximum rate of pay. A listing without a pay range is one of the more
      common reasons a Job Check comes back.
  - title: When advertising is not required
    detail: >-
      Roles on the Green List that meet the Green List requirements, roles paying at least
      $70.00 an hour, and roles on the Global Workforce Seasonal Visa jobs list. Advertising is
      also not required following a business sale or restructure.
    figure:
      value: $70.00 an hour
      label: Advertising exemption threshold
      note: Reported as twice the median wage, so it moves when the median does.
  - title: Work and Income engagement at skill levels 4 and 5
    detail: >-
      For lower-skilled roles you must engage in good faith with Work and Income, which means
      genuinely interviewing suitable candidates they refer. It can run alongside your
      advertising rather than after it. Keep the completed form, the listing, and the
      correspondence — Immigration New Zealand can ask for all three.
  - title: Pay at the market rate
    detail: >-
      This is where the rate is actually tested. Pay must meet the adult minimum wage and the
      market rate for the role in that region — what a New Zealander doing the same work would
      earn. There has been no median wage requirement on the AEWV since 10 March 2025, which
      leads some employers to assume the minimum wage is the whole test. It is not.

process:
  - actor: You
    title: Confirm the role's skill level
    detail: >-
      The skill level sets the advertising period and decides whether Work and Income engagement
      is required. Get it wrong and the advertising is invalid before you start.
  - actor: You
    title: Check whether the role is exempt
    detail: >-
      Green List roles meeting their requirements, roles paying at least $70.00 an hour, and
      GWSV list roles do not need advertising. Worth checking before you spend two or three
      weeks advertising unnecessarily.
  - actor: You
    title: Advertise
    detail: >-
      Fourteen or 21 days depending on skill level, with title, location, duties and a pay
      range. Screenshot the live listing — you will need evidence, and listings disappear.
    duration: 14 or 21 days
  - actor: You
    title: Engage with Work and Income
    detail: >-
      Required at skill levels 4 and 5, and it can run at the same time as the advertising. Keep
      records of referrals and interviews, not just the form.
  - actor: You
    title: Lodge the Job Check
    detail: >-
      Within 90 days of the advertisement closing. One application can cover several jobs where
      the details, advertising and employment agreement are the same.
    duration: Within 90 days
  - actor: Immigration NZ
    title: Assessment
    detail: >-
      Whether you genuinely tested the local labour market, and whether the pay is at market
      rate. Approval lasts six months from that date.
    duration: Valid 6 months
  - actor: Your candidate
    title: Job token and visa application
    detail: >-
      You issue the job token from the approved Job Check, and your candidate applies. A token
      tied to the wrong role or location has to be reissued.

whatGoesWrong:
  - problem: An advertisement missing the pay range
    consequence: >-
      Title, location, duties and a minimum and maximum rate of pay are all required. A listing
      without the range fails, and the advertising period has to run again from the start.
    fix: Check the listing against the requirements before it goes live, not after it closes.
  - problem: Advertising for the wrong period
    consequence: >-
      Fourteen days at skill levels 1 to 3, 21 at levels 4 and 5. Advertising a lower-skilled
      role for a fortnight invalidates the whole exercise.
    fix: Confirm the skill level first. It sets both the period and the Work and Income obligation.
  - problem: Missing the 90-day window
    consequence: >-
      The advertising has to be redone from scratch, adding weeks to a timeline your candidate
      is already counting on.
    fix: Diarise the closing date and lodge well inside the window.
  - problem: Minimum wage mistaken for market rate
    consequence: >-
      Since the median wage requirement was removed, employers read the minimum wage as the
      whole test. A rate below market sinks the role for everyone you want to hire into it.
    fix: >-
      Benchmark against comparable New Zealand appointments in the same region before lodging,
      not after a decline.
  - problem: Work and Income engagement treated as a form
    consequence: >-
      Good faith means interviewing suitable referred candidates. A declaration with nothing
      behind it is an audit risk as well as a Job Check risk.
    fix: Keep the form, the listing, the correspondence and a record of every interview.
  - problem: Bundling roles that are not actually identical
    consequence: >-
      One Job Check covers multiple jobs only where details, advertising and employment
      agreement match. Differences in location or pay split them apart at assessment.
    fix: If any material term differs, lodge separately rather than hoping.

faq:
  - q: How long is an approved Job Check valid?
    a: >-
      Six months from approval, or until your accreditation lapses — whichever comes first. That
      catches employers whose accreditation expires mid-recruitment.
  - q: Can one Job Check cover several hires?
    a: >-
      Yes, where the job details, the advertising and the employment agreement are the same. If
      location, duties or pay differ, they are separate Job Checks.
  - q: When do I not have to advertise?
    a: >-
      Green List roles meeting their requirements, roles paying at least $70.00 an hour, roles on
      the Global Workforce Seasonal Visa list, and after a genuine business sale or restructure.
  - q: Do I have to engage with Work and Income?
    a: >-
      Only for roles at skill level 4 or 5, and it can run alongside your advertising. It must be
      in good faith, which includes interviewing suitable candidates they refer.
  - q: What counts as the market rate?
    a: >-
      What a New Zealander would be paid for the same work in the same region. It is assessed on
      the evidence you supply, not against a single published figure, which is what makes it the
      harder of the two pay tests.
  - q: My Job Check was declined. What now?
    a: >-
      Reconsideration costs $250, but it is only worth lodging if the decision turned on
      something you can now evidence. Where the issue is rate or advertising, fixing the
      underlying problem and reapplying is usually faster.
