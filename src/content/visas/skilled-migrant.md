whoItsFor:
  lead: >-
    Points-led rather than occupation-led. Unlike the Green List, there is no list your job has
    to appear on — but there is a threshold you have to reach, and part-way is the same as
    nothing.
  fits:
    - You have a recognised qualification, occupational registration, or high income.
    - You have New Zealand skilled work experience, or will have.
    - Your occupation is not on the Green List, or is on Tier 2 and you want a faster route.
    - You are 55 or younger.
  doesntFit:
    - You have none of a qualification, registration or income at the required level.
    - Your New Zealand work is not at a skilled level for your occupation.
    - You are over 55.
    - You need residence immediately and have no New Zealand work history.

eligibility:
  - title: Six points, and where they come from
    detail: >-
      Points come from a qualification, occupational registration, or income — then New Zealand
      skilled work experience tops up the total. A doctorate is 6 on its own. A New Zealand
      master's studied full-time for at least 30 weeks is 6. A bachelor's degree is 5 for a
      New Zealand qualification and 4 for an overseas one, so most degree holders need work
      experience to reach six.
    figure:
      value: 6 points
      label: Required to apply
      source: https://www.immigration.govt.nz/visas/skilled-migrant-category-resident-visa/
  - title: Points from registration or income instead
    detail: >-
      Occupational registration earns points on years of experience — six years gives 6 points,
      five gives 5, four gives 4, two gives 3. Income is measured in multiples of the SMC wage
      threshold: three times gives 6 points, twice gives 4, and one and a half times gives 3.
      You claim from one source, not several added together.
    figure:
      value: 3× threshold
      label: Income for a full 6 points
      note: Around $105.00 an hour at the current threshold. Verify the multiplier and the base rate together.
  - title: New Zealand skilled work experience
    detail: >-
      Two years of skilled work in New Zealand gives 3 points, 18 months gives 2, and one year
      gives 1. This is what closes the gap for the many applicants sitting at four or five
      points on qualification alone. The work has to be skilled at the level your occupation
      requires — hours in the country are not the test.
    figure:
      value: 2 years = 3 points
      label: New Zealand skilled work experience
  - title: The Skilled Work Experience pathway
    detail: >-
      Opened 24 August 2026 for people in skilled roles at skill level 1 to 3 with at least
      five years of directly relevant experience, including two years in New Zealand earning at
      least 1.1 times the SMC wage threshold. It exists for experienced people without a
      qualifying degree.
    figure:
      value: 5 years, 2 in NZ
      label: Skilled Work Experience pathway
      effective: 24 August 2026
      note: New Zealand years must be paid at 1.1× the threshold or above.
      source: https://www.immigration.govt.nz/about-us/news-centre/further-changes-to-the-skilled-migrant-category-to-come-into-effect-in-august-2026/
  - title: The Trades and Technician pathway
    detail: >-
      Also from 24 August 2026, for specified trades and technician occupations at skill level
      1 to 3. Requires a relevant Level 4 or higher qualification and at least four years of
      post-qualification experience, including 18 months in New Zealand paid at or above the
      SMC wage threshold.
    figure:
      value: 4 years, 18 months in NZ
      label: Trades and Technician pathway
      effective: 24 August 2026
      source: https://www.immigration.govt.nz/live/resident-visas-to-live-in-new-zealand/skilled-residence-pathways-in-new-zealand/skilled-migrant-category-pathway-to-residence/eligible-roles-for-the-smc-trades-and-technician-pathway-august-2026/
  - title: One wage threshold, and a grace period
    detail: >-
      Before August 2026 applicants had to meet one rate while accruing experience and a higher
      one at residence. Now the threshold is generally the one in force when you started
      accruing skilled work experience, not the one at invitation. If you start work within
      five months of your work visa being granted, the rate at grant applies even if the median
      has risen since.
  - title: Age, English, health and character
    detail: >-
      You must be 55 or younger. English is required for you and for any partner or dependants
      aged 16 and over. Medicals and chest x-rays are required from age 15, police certificates
      under six months old, and partner and dependent children aged 24 or younger can be
      included.

process:
  - actor: You
    title: Count your points honestly
    detail: >-
      Establish which single source you are claiming from, then what New Zealand experience
      adds. Most declines at this stage are arithmetic done hopefully rather than carefully.
  - actor: You
    title: Get the qualification assessed
    detail: >-
      Overseas qualifications generally need assessment against the New Zealand framework
      before they can be relied on. Start it early; it is slow and it gates everything after it.
    duration: Allow 6–12 weeks
  - actor: You
    title: Accrue the New Zealand experience
    detail: >-
      Held at the right skill level and above the applicable wage threshold. Which threshold
      applies depends on when you started, so record that date and keep payslips from day one.
    duration: 1–2 years, depending on points needed
  - actor: You
    title: Submit an expression of interest
    detail: >-
      Once you can evidence six points. Claiming points you cannot document is the most common
      way a straightforward case becomes a difficult one.
  - actor: Immigration NZ
    title: Invitation to apply
    detail: >-
      An invitation follows a qualifying expression of interest. Your wage threshold is
      generally the one from when you started accruing experience, not from this moment.
  - actor: You
    title: Lodge the residence application
    detail: >-
      With full evidence for every point claimed, plus health, character and English for
      everyone included.
  - actor: Immigration NZ
    title: Assessment and decision
    detail: A request for further information effectively restarts the clock.

whatGoesWrong:
  - problem: Adding points from several sources at once
    consequence: >-
      Points come from one source — qualification, registration or income — topped up with New
      Zealand work experience. Adding a degree to registration to income produces a number that
      does not exist.
    fix: Establish the single source first, then add experience. If that is under six, you are not ready.
  - problem: An overseas qualification that does not map where you assumed
    consequence: >-
      A degree that seems equivalent may assess below the level claimed, taking the total under
      six after months of planning.
    fix: Get the assessment done before building a timeline on it, not while the application is in train.
  - problem: New Zealand work that is not skilled work
    consequence: >-
      Time in the country is not the test. Work below the skill level for your occupation earns
      no points however long it lasts.
    fix: >-
      Confirm the skill level of the actual role against your occupation before counting a year
      of it toward anything.
  - problem: Not knowing when your experience clock started
    consequence: >-
      Since August 2026 the applicable wage threshold is generally the one in force when you
      began accruing skilled experience. People who cannot evidence that date lose the benefit
      of the older, lower rate.
    fix: >-
      Record the start date and keep payslips from the first week. If you started within five
      months of your visa being granted, the grant-date rate may apply instead.
  - problem: Advice written before 24 August 2026
    consequence: >-
      The two-threshold rule, the absence of the trades pathway and the old experience
      requirements were all correct until then. Plans built on them now fail for reasons that
      are invisible unless you know the date the rules moved.
    fix: Check the date of any guidance you are relying on, including ours.
  - problem: Ageing past 55 mid-pathway
    consequence: >-
      Someone accruing two years of experience at 54 has no Skilled Migrant route left when it
      completes.
    fix: Work backwards from your 56th birthday before committing to an experience-based plan.

faq:
  - q: Do I need exactly six points, or more?
    a: >-
      Six is the threshold to apply. More does not accelerate anything, and five means you are
      not eligible yet — usually a question of one more year of New Zealand skilled work.
  - q: Can I combine a degree and registration to reach six?
    a: >-
      No. You claim from one source, then add New Zealand skilled work experience on top. That
      catches out a lot of otherwise strong applicants.
  - q: What changed on 24 August 2026?
    a: >-
      Three things: most applicants now meet a single wage threshold rather than one for
      experience and a higher one at residence; a five-month grace period fixes your threshold
      to your visa grant date; and two pathways opened for people without a qualifying degree,
      one for five years of skilled experience and one for trades and technician roles.
  - q: I have no degree. Is there a route?
    a: >-
      Since August 2026, yes. The Skilled Work Experience pathway needs five years of directly
      relevant experience including two in New Zealand at 1.1 times the threshold. The Trades
      and Technician pathway needs a Level 4 qualification and four years post-qualification,
      including 18 months in New Zealand at the threshold.
  - q: Is Skilled Migrant slower than the Green List?
    a: >-
      Slower than Green List Tier 1, usually. Against Tier 2's 24 months it can be competitive,
      particularly if you already hold New Zealand experience. Worth comparing both rather than
      assuming.
  - q: Can my family be included?
    a: >-
      Your partner and dependent children aged 24 or younger can be included. Everyone aged 16
      and over has English requirements, and everyone needs medicals and police certificates.
