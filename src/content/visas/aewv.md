intro: >-
  The main route for skilled workers, and the visa most often lost on a technicality
  rather than on merit. Three approvals have to line up — your employer's accreditation,
  a Job Check for the role, and your own application. A failure at any one stops the
  other two.

whoItsFor:
  lead: >-
    An employer-led visa. Without a named employer, role and location, there is nothing
    to apply for.
  fits:
    - You have a full-time offer from a New Zealand employer, or are close.
    - You have two years' experience, or a qualification in the same field.
    - You need New Zealand work experience on the record for residence.
    - Your employer is accredited, or will commit to it.
  doesntFit:
    - You want to arrive first and look for work. It is tied to one employer.
    - You are self-employed, or will contract to several clients.
    - You expect your partner and children on the same application.
    - You are near your maximum stay with no stand-down plan.

eligibility:
  - title: A full-time offer from an accredited employer
    detail: >-
      Accreditation must already be held, and the offer must guarantee at least 30 hours a
      week. Check the published accredited employer list yourself before you resign.
    figure:
      value: 30 hours a week
      label: Minimum guaranteed hours
      source: https://www.immigration.govt.nz/visas/accredited-employer-work-visa/
  - title: "Skill: two years' experience or a level 4 qualification"
    detail: >-
      Two years' relevant experience, or a qualification at NZQCF level 4 or above, in the
      same field. Bachelor's degree holders can apply in any field. Experience must be
      evidenced by someone other than you — references backed by certificates, payslips
      or tax records.
    figure:
      value: 2 years, or NZQCF level 4
      label: Skill threshold
      source: https://www.immigration.govt.nz/visas/accredited-employer-work-visa/
  - title: Market rate pay, and at least the minimum wage
    detail: >-
      No median wage requirement since 10 March 2025. Pay must meet the adult minimum wage
      and the market rate — what a New Zealander would earn for the same work in the same
      region. Market rate is the harder test, assessed on the employer's evidence at Job
      Check rather than a published number.
    figure:
      value: $23.95 an hour
      label: Adult minimum wage
      effective: 1 April 2026
  - title: English language, for skill level 3 and below
    detail: >-
      Required at skill levels 3, 4 and 5; the extension to level 3 took effect on 1 June
      2026. Results must be under two years old. Exemptions cover certain citizenships
      with five years' work or study, comparable degrees from Australia, Canada, Ireland,
      New Zealand, the UK or the USA, an AEWV expiring on or before 1 December 2026, and
      anyone who already gave English evidence on an earlier AEWV.
    figure:
      value: IELTS 4.0 overall
      label: Minimum test scores
      note: Equivalents reported as TOEFL iBT 31, PTE Academic 29, B2 First 142. OET for healthcare roles.
      source: https://www.immigration.govt.nz/process-to-apply/applying-for-a-visa/providing-evidence-and-documents-to-support-your-visa-application/english-language-requirements/english-language-requirements-for-an-accredited-employer-work-visa/
  - title: Health and character
    detail: >-
      A chest x-ray for stays over six months from high-TB countries. Police certificates,
      under six months old, where your total stay reaches 24 months. Character issues are
      cheaper to address before lodging than after a decline.

process:
  - actor: Your employer
    title: Employer accreditation
    detail: >-
      Standard for up to five migrant workers, high-volume for six or more; franchisee and
      triangular categories cover franchise and labour-hire arrangements.
    duration: Initial grant lasts 12 months
  - actor: Your employer
    title: Advertising the role
    detail: >-
      At least two weeks before the Job Check, unless exempt. At skill level 4 or 5 it is
      21 days plus good-faith engagement with Work and Income — genuinely interviewing
      suitable referred candidates, not just lodging a form.
    duration: 2 weeks, or 21 days at skill level 4–5
  - actor: Your employer
    title: Job Check
    detail: >-
      Per role, not per person, lodged within 90 days of the advertisement closing. This
      is where market rate is actually assessed.
    duration: Valid 6 months
  - actor: Your employer
    title: Job token issued to you
    detail: >-
      Sent once the Job Check is approved. Nothing can be lodged without it; a token tied
      to the wrong role or location must be reissued.
  - actor: You
    title: Your visa application
    detail: >-
      Lodged online with the job token, evidence of skill and experience, English evidence
      if required, and health and character documents.
  - actor: Immigration NZ
    title: Assessment and decision
    detail: >-
      Assessed against the approved Job Check. A request for further information
      effectively restarts the clock.
    duration: See timeline below

    headline: From $1,540

      The visa fee and immigration levy. Add a medical examination and police
      certificates, which are paid to third parties, not to Immigration New Zealand.
  - payer: Your employer
    headline: $1,510 – $4,795
    detail: >-
      Accreditation plus one Job Check. The low end is standard accreditation, the
      high end triangular. Accreditation is paid once and covers further hires;
      the Job Check fee recurs per role.

whatGoesWrong:
  - problem: Signing before checking accreditation
    consequence: >-
      People resign, sometimes relocate, on an offer from an employer who is not
      accredited and has no intention of applying. Nothing can be lodged.
    fix: >-
      Check the accredited employer list yourself first. If they are not on it, ask for a
      written commitment, with a date.
  - problem: Experience evidenced only by your own account
    consequence: >-
      A reference letter with no certificates, payslips or tax records behind it is
      routinely treated as insufficient, and the application stalls.
    fix: >-
      Assemble independent records before lodging. Where an employer no longer exists, tax
      and banking records carry more weight.
  - problem: Minimum wage mistaken for market rate
    consequence: >-
      Employers read the removal of the median wage as meaning the minimum wage is the
      whole test. A rate below market sinks the role for everyone they want to hire.
    fix: >-
      Benchmark against comparable New Zealand appointments in the region before the Job
      Check, not after a decline.
  - problem: Work and Income engagement treated as paperwork
    consequence: >-
      At levels 4 and 5 engagement must be in good faith, including interviewing suitable
      referred candidates. An unsupported declaration is an audit risk too.
    fix: >-
      Keep the form, the listing, the correspondence and a record of interviews.
  - problem: No plan for the maximum continuous stay
    consequence: >-
      People reach the end of their stay with no residence application in train, then face
      12 months outside New Zealand.
    fix: >-
      Work backwards from the maximum stay on day one. Qualifying experience must accrue
      from the start.
  - problem: Assuming the family travels with you
    consequence: >-
      Partners and children cannot be included, and whether you can support them turns on
      your role's skill level.
    fix: Assess the partner and child applications alongside yours, not after yours is granted.

faq:
  - q: Can I change employers on an AEWV?
    a: >-
      Not freely. The visa is tied to the employer, role and location named on it. A new
      employer means a further application, supported by their accreditation and Job
      Check. Starting before approval puts your visa at risk.
  - q: Can my partner and children come with me?
    a: >-
      Not on your application, but they can apply in their own right. Supporting a
      partner's work visa depends on your role's skill level and an income threshold tied
      to the median wage. Dependent children can generally study as domestic students.
  - q: Does time on an AEWV count toward residence?
    a: >-
      Yes, and that is usually the point of it. Skilled employment held continuously is
      the evidence most residence applications turn on. Choose the first visa with
      residence already in mind.
  - q: What happens when I reach the maximum stay?
    a: >-
      Five years for most roles; three at skill levels 4 and 5. After that, 12 months
      outside New Zealand before a further AEWV. Roles paying at least 1.5 times the
      median wage have been treated as qualifying for five years regardless of skill level
      — confirm before relying on it.
  - q: My employer will pay the minimum wage. Is that enough?
    a: >-
      It meets the floor, not the test. Pay must also be at market rate for the role in
      that region. Materially below what a New Zealander would earn is a problem at Job
      Check — and a reason to ask questions before accepting.
  - q: How long does the whole thing take?
    a: >-
      Three to five months from offer to decision if the employer is not yet accredited.
      An accredited employer with a current Job Check is much faster — two of the three
      approvals already exist.
