whoItsFor:
  lead: >-
    This is the temporary route — a partner joining someone who holds a work visa. If your
    partner is a New Zealand citizen or resident, you are looking at partnership residence
    instead.
  fits:
    - Your partner holds a New Zealand work visa longer than six months.
    - Their role and pay clear the support threshold for their skill level.
    - You have been living together and can evidence it.
    - You want open work rights rather than a job offer of your own.
  doesntFit:
    - Your partner is a New Zealand citizen or resident — partnership residence applies.
    - Your partner holds a Working Holiday, Fishing Crew or Seasonal Employer visa.
    - You have not lived together and have little documentary history.
    - You already have your own job offer, which may make an AEWV the stronger route.

eligibility:
  - title: Your partner holds a qualifying work visa
    detail: >-
      It must run longer than six months. Working Holiday, Fishing Crew and Seasonal Employer
      visas cannot support a partner. The exception runs the other way for a Migrant
      Exploitation Protection Work Visa, which can support even when short.
  - title: Their pay clears the threshold for their skill level
    detail: >-
      This is the requirement that decides most of these applications. At skill levels 1 to 3
      the supporting partner must earn at least $28.00 an hour. At levels 4 and 5 it jumps to
      $52.50 — unless the role is on the Green List, where $35.00 applies, or falls in the care
      or transport sectors, where it is $28.00 or the sector rate, whichever is higher.
    figure:
      value: $28.00 / $52.50 / $35.00
      label: Support thresholds by skill level and list
      effective: 9 March 2026
      note: Levels 1–3 / levels 4–5 / Green List at levels 4–5. Verify against the role's current classification.
      source: https://www.immigration.govt.nz/visas/partner-of-a-worker-work-visa/
  - title: A genuine and stable relationship, living together
    detail: >-
      Immigration New Zealand assesses how long you have been together and living together,
      whether finances and responsibilities are shared, and whether others recognise the
      relationship. This is a documentary test, not a question of sincerity — which is why
      couples who are plainly genuine still get declined.
  - title: What the evidence actually has to show
    detail: >-
      Tenancy agreements and bills in both names, joint accounts or consistent transfers,
      correspondence to a shared address, photographs across the span of the relationship
      rather than one occasion, travel together, and statements from people who know you both.
      Volume matters less than continuity over time.
  - title: Health and character
    detail: >-
      A chest x-ray if you will be here more than six months and are from a country with a high
      incidence of tuberculosis. Police certificates where your total stay will reach 24 months.
      Your supporting partner must also meet character requirements.
  - title: What you get
    detail: >-
      Open work rights — any employer, any role, anywhere in New Zealand, with no job offer and
      no Job Check. The visa runs for the same period as your partner's, so it inherits their
      expiry rather than having one of its own.

process:
  - actor: Your employer
    title: Confirm the supporting role first
    detail: >-
      Establish the skill level and the exact hourly rate before anything else. A role at
      skill level 4 paying $40.00 an hour supports nobody, and finding that out after lodging
      is an expensive way to learn it.
  - actor: You
    title: Assemble the relationship evidence
    detail: >-
      Work backwards across the whole relationship rather than gathering what is close to hand.
      Gaps in the timeline are what draw questions.
    duration: Allow 2–4 weeks
  - actor: You
    title: Lodge the application
    detail: >-
      With your partner's visa details, evidence of their pay and role, your relationship
      evidence, and health and character documents.
  - actor: Immigration NZ
    title: Assessment
    detail: >-
      Both limbs are assessed: the supporting partner's eligibility to support, and the
      relationship itself. A request for further information usually lands on the second.
    duration: 80% within 5.5 weeks

whatGoesWrong:
  - problem: Pay a few dollars under the threshold
    consequence: >-
      A supporting partner at skill level 4 on $45.00 an hour is well paid and still cannot
      support anyone, because the threshold there is $52.50. Couples discover this after lodging.
    fix: >-
      Check the skill level and the rate together before applying. If the role is close to a
      Green List entry, that reclassification can drop the threshold to $35.00.
  - problem: Evidence that starts when the relationship got serious
    consequence: >-
      Couples produce six months of joint bills and nothing before it. The instructions look at
      the duration of the relationship, so a short documentary trail reads as a short relationship.
    fix: >-
      Go back to the beginning — early messages, flights, photographs, anything dated. Continuity
      across time carries more weight than volume from one period.
  - problem: Living together that cannot be documented
    consequence: >-
      Sharing a home with everything in one name leaves nothing to show. This is common and
      entirely fixable, but not retrospectively.
    fix: >-
      Get both names onto the tenancy, a utility account and any insurance now, so the record
      exists by the time you need it.
  - problem: Assuming the visa outlasts the partner's
    consequence: >-
      It is granted for the same period as the supporting visa. When theirs is renewed late or
      declined, yours does not survive independently.
    fix: Treat the two applications as one timeline and renew together, early.
  - problem: The supporting partner's visa type is excluded
    consequence: >-
      Working Holiday, Fishing Crew and Seasonal Employer visas cannot support a partner at all,
      regardless of pay.
    fix: >-
      Check the visa type before the pay rate. If it is excluded, the route is a visa in your own
      right rather than a partner application.
  - problem: Treating it as a formality because the relationship is obvious
    consequence: >-
      Most partnership declines are evidentiary rather than factual. Being genuinely together is
      necessary and not sufficient.
    fix: Prepare it as a file to be assessed by someone who has never met you, because it will be.

faq:
  - q: Can I work on this visa?
    a: >-
      Yes, and openly — any employer, any role, anywhere in New Zealand. No job offer, no Job
      Check, no job token. It is one of the more useful visas in the system for that reason.
  - q: How long is it granted for?
    a: >-
      The same period as your partner's work visa. It inherits their expiry, so the two renewals
      have to be planned together.
  - q: How long do we need to have been living together?
    a: >-
      There is no fixed minimum for this temporary visa in the way there is for residence, where
      12 months applies. What matters is that the relationship is genuine and stable and that you
      are living together, evidenced across its duration.
  - q: My partner earns well but the role is skill level 4. Is that a problem?
    a: >-
      Possibly. At levels 4 and 5 the threshold is $52.50 an hour rather than $28.00, unless the
      role is on the Green List or in the care or transport sectors. Well paid is not the same as
      above the threshold.
  - q: Can our children come too?
    a: >-
      They apply separately as dependent children. Whether they can study as domestic students
      depends on the visa you hold, which is worth assessing at the same time rather than after.
  - q: What if we have not lived together yet?
    a: >-
      That is a difficult application on this route. Depending on your circumstances a visitor
      visa, or your own work visa, may be the more realistic first step. Worth an assessment
      before you spend the fee.
