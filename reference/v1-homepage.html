Nobody arrives on a resident visa

Residence is the end of a sequence, not the start of one. Knowing which stage you're at, and what the next one demands, is most of the work.

01

Get here lawfully

A work, student or visitor visa with conditions you can actually meet. The wrong first visa quietly closes doors two years later, and by then it is expensive to undo.

Typically 4–10 weeks
02

Build the record

Skilled employment at the right wage, held continuously. This is the evidence every residence decision is actually made on, and it is built long before you apply.

Typically 2 years
03

Apply for residence

Skilled Migrant Category, Green List, or a partnership-based application. Each is assessed on different grounds, and choosing the wrong one costs you months.

Typically 6–12 months
04

Make it permanent

A resident visa carries travel conditions. A permanent resident visa does not. It is the step people forget, and the one that strands them offshore when it lapses.

After 2 years as a resident
